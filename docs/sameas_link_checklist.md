## How to verify it worked

After each change, the link is live immediately on the profile site, but Google takes anywhere from a few days to a few weeks to recrawl those pages and update its entity graph. You don't need to check back; just do them and move on.

## Why this matters

Each reciprocal link is a small vote that strengthens Google's confidence that all these online identities are the same person. That confidence is one of the inputs for:
- Showing your photo and bio in the right sidebar of Google search results for your name
- Eventually triggering a Google Knowledge Panel
- Better surfacing in AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity) when someone asks about you or your research areas
